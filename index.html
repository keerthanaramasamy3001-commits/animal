🐯 Lion Fact Sheet

Lions are one of the most powerful and majestic animals in the world. Known as the "King of the Jungle," they live in groups called prides and are famous for their strength, courage, and loud roar. Lions are found mainly in Africa and play an important role in maintaining the balance of nature.

Scientific Name

Panthera leo

Habitat

Grasslands, Savannas, and Open Woodlands

Diet

Carnivore (Meat Eater)

Lifespan

10–14 Years in the Wild

Speed

Up to 80 km/h

Weight

120–250 kg

🌟 Fun Fact

🌟 A lion's roar can be heard up to 8 kilometers away! 🦁
